Volunteer handbook

Thank you for giving your time to the summer programs. This handbook sets out what to expect on your first day, and who to ask when you need help. The volunteer schedule has your shifts.

Your first day

Arrive fifteen minutes before your shift, and sign in at the front desk. A program lead will show you around and introduce you to the group.

  • Bring your volunteer badge
  • Wear closed-toe shoes
  • Leave valuables at home

Signing in

  1. Show your badge at the front desk
  2. Check the board for your room
  3. Meet your program lead there
Every child is signed out by a parent or guardian, or by someone they have named on the form.

If something goes wrong

Tell your program lead first. For anything urgent, call the office, or report an incident online.

First aid

Each room has a first aid kit by the door. The leads are trained in first aid.
