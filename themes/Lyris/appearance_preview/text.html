Cataloguing artifacts

Thank you for helping to look after the collections. This guide sets out how to record an artifact when it arrives, and who to ask when you need help. The list of collections shows where each artifact can go.

Recording a new artifact

Measure the artifact before you start, and have the donor's details to hand. A curator checks each new record before the artifact can appear in an exhibit.

  • Give it a short name
  • Record its height, width and depth in cm
  • Rate its condition from 1 to 10

Recording a donation

  1. Look for the donor in the list of donors
  2. Add them if they aren't there yet
  3. Choose them as the artifact's donor
Every artifact belongs to at least one collection, so that curators can find it when they plan an exhibit.

If something goes wrong

Tell a curator first. For anything urgent, call the office, or report damage to an artifact online.

Handling artifacts

Wear gloves when you handle an artifact, and hold it with both hands.
