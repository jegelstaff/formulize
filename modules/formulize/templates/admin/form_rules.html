<{* The Rules tab of a form: rules, notifications from the notifications popup, and background work *}> <{* form elements must be named with their object name hyphen field name, other elements must not have hyphens *}>

Rules say what should happen automatically when something happens to entries in this form: when an entry is created, updated, saved or deleted, or a custom button is clicked, which entries it applies to, and what happens, in steps: send a notification, update or create entries, ask AI, send a webhook to another system, or run code. Later steps can use what earlier steps produced.

Rules that ask AI run in the background, after the page that triggered them has been sent. Webhooks are sent right after the event. Both are retried with increasing delays (1 minute, 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 hours, 12 hours) if they fail. Retries happen every few minutes while people are using the site, when you click "Do them now" or "Send now" here, or when a cron job calls <{$smarty.const.XOOPS_URL}>/formulize-public-api/v1/queue/all/process.
