It is deliberately theme-agnostic: it pulls in the current theme's stylesheet by asking xoops_getcss() rather than hard-coding a path, and it makes no assumptions about that theme's class names or layout. The trade-off is that a theme whose page styling depends on wrapper markup in its own theme.html will look plainer here than it does on the website.
