# The menu

The menu down the side of every page lists your applications, each with the links inside it. It
can also have links of its own beside the applications, to a form, a screen or any web address,
such as a help page or a dashboard that everyone uses.

People only see the links that are shown to their groups. An application with no links that they
can see isn't in their menu at all.

You manage the menu in two places:

- **Admin > Menu** puts the applications in order, and holds the links beside them.
- **An application's Menu Entries tab** holds the links inside that application, and their order.
  Open it from the application's row on **Admin > Apps**, or from **Manage this application's menu
  entries** on the Menu tab.

## Putting the menu in order

On **Admin > Menu**, under **What's in the menu**, the applications and the links beside them are
listed in the order they appear in the menu.

1. Drag an item up or down by its handle, the six dots at its left. With the keyboard, press Tab to
   reach the handle, then use the up and down arrow keys. Home and End move it to the top or the
   bottom.
2. Click **Save your changes**.

Click an item's name to open it. An application shows the links inside it, where each one goes, and
which groups see it. You change those on its Menu Entries tab.

**General Forms** holds the links to forms that aren't in any application. It's in the list, and in
the menu, only while it has links, and you can put it anywhere.

A new application goes at the end of the menu until you move it.

## Adding a link beside the applications

1. On **Admin > Menu**, open **Add a link to the menu**.
2. Type the **Text for this link**, or leave it blank to use the name of the form or screen.
3. Under **This link goes to**, choose **A form or screen** and pick one from the list, or choose
   **A URL** and type the address in the box that appears.
4. Under **Show this link to these groups**, tick every group that should see it. A link shown to
   no groups isn't shown to anyone, not even webmasters. To show it to everyone with an account,
   tick **Registered Users**.
5. Click **Add the link**.

The link goes at the end of the menu. Drag it to where you want it, and save your changes.

To change a link, open it in the list, change what you need, and click **Save your changes**. To
remove it, click **Delete this link**. Deleting a link only takes it out of the menu: the form,
screen or page it went to is still there.

## Sending people to a page when they log in

A link can be the page a group lands on right after logging in. Under **Send these groups to this
link right after they log in**, tick the groups. This works for links beside the applications and
for links inside them.

- A group only lands on a link it can see, so tick it under **Show this link to these groups** too.
- It only works for groups whose start page is Formulize, under **General Settings** in the system
  admin.
- If someone belongs to several groups that land on different links, they go to the one highest in
  the menu. Moving an application, or a link beside the applications, up or down the menu changes
  which one that is.

## If something isn't right

- **An application isn't in the menu.** It has no links that the person's groups can see. On the
  Menu tab it says **No links, so it isn't in the menu**. Add links on its Menu Entries tab, and show
  them to the right groups.
- **A message says the menu changed since the page was loaded, and the new order wasn't saved.**
  Someone else added or deleted an application or link while you were working, perhaps in another
  tab. The page reloads with the menu as it is now. Put it in order again, and save.
- **A link to a form isn't in the menu.** The form may have been deleted. Links to forms that no
  longer exist are left out of the menu. Delete the link, or point it somewhere else.
