# Non-profit and individual pricing

Formulize itself is free for everybody, forever. These reductions apply to hosting and to services from the Formulize development team.

*Pricing preview — these numbers are still being finalized.*

## Non-profit organizations

| Plan | Standard | Non-profit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Silver | $1,199/month | $999/month |
| Gold | $1,899/month | $1,499/month |
| Diamond | $2,299/month | $1,799/month |
| Platinum | $8,999/month | $6,999/month |
| Support and consulting by the hour | $200/hour | $150/hour |

## Individuals

For one person working on their own data, rather than an organization.

| Plan | Standard | Individual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Trio | $149/month | $79/month |
| Bronze | $199/month | $99/month |

## How to claim it

[Get in touch through formulize.net](https://formulize.net){:target="_blank"} and tell us which plan you want and which reduction applies. There is no form to fill in and no paperwork to send us up front.
