Twenty years of other people's data

Formulize is software for collecting and organising information, built in the open since 2005 and given away under the GPL. It is maintained by a small team who also host it, train people on it, and fix it when it breaks.

Nobody has ever had to pay to use it, and nobody ever will.

formulize.org and formulize.net

Two addresses, one team. It is worth being clear about which is which.

This site

formulize.org

The open source project. The software, the documentation, the release history, the roadmap and the community. Everything here is free, and the source is on GitHub under GPL-2.0.

Formulize on GitHub
The other one

formulize.net

The hosting and services side, run by the same people. If you would rather not run a server yourself, or you want training and support, or help configuring your system, those are the services you are buying. Formulize itself is always free.

Nothing gets stranded

Where your data lives

On a hosted plan, your data stays in Canada, on every plan, with backups every night. How far back those restore points reach depends on your level. If your organisation has particular requirements — a records retention schedule, a procurement checklist, a written response commitment — those can be built into your arrangement during setup.

If you self-host, none of this applies: the data is on your server, under your rules, and we never see it.
