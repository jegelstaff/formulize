One system, configured your way

With Formulize, you can create web-based forms, connect them together to make unique apps, and publish the data with interactive reports. It is quickly configured, and reconfigured, so it adapts as your needs change and your data grows.

Formulize is 100% configuration based. There is no code to write, and nothing to recompile — which is also why an AI assistant can understand your whole system and help you change it.

If you would rather see it than read about it, here are six things people build with it.

Improvise. Organize. Customize.

All of it is in every copy of Formulize, hosted or self-hosted. Nothing is held back for paying customers.

  • Create any kind of database application

Organize
