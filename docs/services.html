We're here to help

Formulize is designed to be used by anyone, but that doesn't mean you have to figure it out by yourself.

The same people who write the software can build you a system, move your data in, train your staff, and answer the phone whenever you've got questions.

Available on any plan — including the free download.

What we do

Getting going

Getting started with Formulize

An orientation session for you and your colleagues: what Formulize is, how it fits your situation, and what your first forms should be. Often this is all anybody needs.

Training

Training your people

From an hour to several days, for the people who will build forms and for the people who will only ever fill them in. A day means eight hours of our time, and usually works better as two mornings than one long sitting.

Design

System design and implementation

Tell us what the work actually looks like and we will design the forms, the connections between them and the permissions to match, then build it with you so you can keep changing it yourself afterwards.

Your old data

Data migration

Spreadsheets, an old database, a system nobody has the password for any more. We move what is worth moving, and tell you honestly what is not.

When it breaks

Tech support

Staffed 8am to 6pm Eastern, Monday to Friday. On the hosted plans it is unlimited and comes with a response time guarantee; for everyone else we can still help by the hour.

AI

Using AI with your data

We can help you connect an AI assistant, control what it should and should not be allowed to do, and figure out where it genuinely saves you time. Read how AI works in Formulize.
