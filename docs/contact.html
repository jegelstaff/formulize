Tell us what you are trying to do

You do not need to know what to ask for. Describe the situation in your own words — what you are tracking, roughly how many people are involved, and what is going wrong now — and we will tell you what it would take.

Sometimes that answer is “download it, it is free, here is the guide”. We would rather tell you that than sell you something you do not need.
