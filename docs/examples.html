Why do people use Formulize?

Off-the-shelf products don't work the way your team actually works

  • Formulize is designed around customization to eliminate the “square peg, round hole” problem.

You have data locked away in foreign systems you don't control

  • Formulize is open source and gives you complete access to the entire system, including all your data.

You've got too many cobbled-together solutions

  • Formulize replaces all the emails, the half-working systems, and the monster spreadsheet that breaks whenever someone edits the wrong cell.

What do people use it for?

People and relationships

Memberships and renewals

The situation: The membership list is a spreadsheet that is mostly up to date, just be careful when you're looking for who needs to renew, because if you don't include every column when you re-sort then everything gets very mixed up.

With Formulize:

  • A member form, with a renewal history joined to it
  • Start by importing the spreadsheet you already have
  • Members can log in and update their own record, so the list keeps itself current
  • A series of live reports, including who is lapsing in the next month

Contact management and CRM

The situation: Everyone keeps good track of their own contacts — but in separate lists, and which one has the new phone number for the big client?

With Formulize:

  • A contacts form, with interactions and notes joined to each one
  • One record per contact, that everyone adds to and everyone can see
  • Segment contacts by group, tag, or status
  • A report of who needs a follow-up, and by whom

Volunteers and shift scheduling

The situation: Which volunteers have completed the new training module? And meanwhile the coordinator can't keep up with requests for swapping shifts, so the schedule is only partly right...

With Formulize:

  • A volunteer profile form that records training and whatever else you need
  • A shift form attached to each profile, and volunteers can pick from available shifts or request swaps
  • Coordinators see the whole schedule; volunteers see only their own shifts
  • Everything is always up to date; easily search profiles and find open shifts

Managing placements and co-ops

The situation: The matching gets done, in a spreadsheet and a lot of email, but the criteria shift every term, and the candidates are always asking about their status...

With Formulize:

  • A candidates form and a placements form, joined together by a third form to record who is assigned where
  • Track preferences, capacity, and requirements to automatically find suitable candidates
  • Coordinators see everything; candidates see only their own assignment
  • For the next term, update only the data that changed; the same process keeps working

Money and resources

Grant applications and reporting

The situation: Applications and progress reports both come in as email attachments, and the only thing connecting them is a carefully named folder.

With Formulize:

  • One form for the application, connected to one for each progress report
  • Everything joined by applicant, so all reports sit where they belong
  • Applicants see only their own entries; staff see all of them
  • A flexible report of what is outstanding and what is due next, by date, or whatever criteria you need right now

Expense claims and approvals

The situation: There's a different claim form depending on the type of expense, and they all get emailed to accounting, which is always behind in saying who is owed how much...

With Formulize:

  • A dynamic claims form that asks the right questions for each kind of expense
  • Staff submit their own; approvers see only what needs their sign-off
  • Running reports of what is approved but not yet paid, and totals by category
  • When it's time to reconcile, export the data to Excel, QuickBooks, etc.

Equipment and room bookings

The situation: Bookings are written into a shared calendar everyone sees, but only one person can edit it, so everyone else has to send requests to that person by email, or phone...

With Formulize:

  • An items form and a bookings form, joined together
  • Anyone can request; certain people can approve
  • One shared list automatically generated, of what is out, who has it, and when it's due back
  • Filter by this month, or a date range, or category, or search for a particular item

Inventory

The situation: There is a stock list, and it was right on the day it was counted, but nothing since then records what has actually moved in or out.

With Formulize:

  • An items form, with a movements form joined to it
  • Anyone can log what came in or went out, as it happens
  • What is on hand is a report, not a recount
  • Search by location, category, or condition, or filter for what needs reordering

Operations and casework

Client intake and case notes

The situation: There are a lot of paper forms at intake, so data entry is a constant, and then the notes system doesn't allow more than one worker per client so we have to duplicate profiles...

With Formulize:

  • An intake form, with case notes joined to each client
  • One client history, added to by whoever is working the case
  • Add and remove workers just by changing permissions; one set of unified data
  • Front-desk staff see all intakes and profile info; caseworkers see their own notes

Activity logging

The situation: Work gets done, and logged, but the logging system doesn't have a place for “category”, so that info gets typed into the general notes box...

With Formulize:

  • A customized log form that anyone on the team can fill in
  • Entries are linked to the client, project, or case they belong to, so reporting is easy
  • A running history that nobody has to piece together manually
  • Filter by person, date, or category, and get automatic calculations of totals

Time tracking

The situation: Everybody fills in a timesheet, but the categorization options aren't as detailed as we need, so admins export to Excel each month...

With Formulize:

  • A customized time entry form, joined to the project or client it is billed to
  • People log their own hours; managers see everyone's, and can classify according to whatever criteria you need
  • Totals by person, project, or date range, at any point in the month
  • For payroll and billing, export the totals or directly integrate with accounting via the API

Assessments and evaluations

The situation: The assessments get done and the scores get recorded, but the system can't compare across years, we're not sure why, so we put the totals from prior years into a spreadsheet to make reports...

With Formulize:

  • One assessment form, joined to the person or program it covers
  • Assessors see only what is assigned to them
  • Results compared across everyone at once, and across any timeframe, including years
  • Filter results by person, program, or date range

Public forms and content

Event registration and RSVPs

The situation: Registrations come in and every one of them gets recorded, but it's hard to track when an event is full, and everything has to be retyped into a single attendance list for the door staff.

With Formulize:

  • A public form where anyone can RSVP, no account needed
  • An events form behind the scenes where each event's RSVPs show up automatically
  • The public form only includes events that still have space; organizers log in and see all events
  • A door list is automatically generated, and you can sort and search it on the day

Surveys and questionnaires

The situation: The survey tool does its job, but the results come back as their own export, sitting apart from the people and programs they are actually about.

With Formulize:

  • A public form for respondents, no account required
  • Responses joined to the person, event, or program they relate to
  • Results reported alongside everything else you track
  • Easily reuse or adapt the same form for the next round

Custom content management

The situation: The website is beautiful and custom-made, but the content is hard to change and we want to add regularly updated announcements that any staff person can write, but only managers approve...

With Formulize:

  • A custom form in Formulize to record the announcements; the website pulls in the content live over the API
  • Editors work in ordinary forms, approvers review and publish; no coding required
  • This site's own News section is a live example of this exact workflow
  • No separate CMS to license, host, or keep in sync

Do any of those sound familiar?

Formulize has helped organizations with all these needs. Memberships might seem different from Activity Logs, but to a database it's all variations on a theme.

Make the forms

  • One form per kind of thing you are tracking
  • Create as many forms as you need, each with any number of entries
  • Or describe them to an AI assistant and let it build them for you

Join them together

  • Each client has many notes; each grant has many reports
  • The connection is a setting, not code
  • Reports can then draw on both forms at once

Decide who sees what

  • Control who sees which entries in which forms
  • Restrict who can create and modify data
  • Publish searchable reports to your users

Formulize gives you complete control by letting you build solutions that meet your needs precisely. You build by clicking, not by coding, so the next time the goalposts move, you just change some settings and the whole system updates automatically.
