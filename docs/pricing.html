Step zero — and it never expires

Open Source. Free forever.

Download Formulize, put it on any PHP and MySQL/MariaDB host, and it is yours. Unlimited forms, unlimited users, unlimited data, AI integration, and free tech support and training through the open source project. GPL-2.0, no strings, no cut-down edition.
