It was time for Formulize users to get together, learn from each other, and redefine how they work with their data. Time for new users to learn from the experts. Time to strengthen the nascent community around this unique software. And time to launch Formulize 7, the completely redesigned and most mobile friendly version yet.

* **Who:** Formulize users of all stripes, and anyone who was curious
* **What:** A day of sharing and learning
* **Where:** [One King West](https://www.onekingwest.com){:target="_blank"}, in the heart of downtown Toronto
* **When:** August 30, 2022, all day
* **Why:** To see what's possible and imagine the future
* **How:** Demos, examples, stories, insights and wishlists — putting our heads together so the whole is greater than the sum of the parts
