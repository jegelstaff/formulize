You can use AI assistants to help you work with Formulize. They can understand the way Formulize is configured, and they can help you create data, update data, analyse it, maintain it, validate it and correct it.

AI can even create forms and elements based on your prompts, or based on documents you upload, or whatever other information you can provide. It can connect multiple forms into useful applications, and update the configuration of the elements too.

## Formulize is ideal for AI

Formulize is the perfect system to use with AI, because Formulize is 100% configuration based. The configuration of the forms, their permissions, their connections to other forms — all of that together completely explains what your system does *and how it does it*. With most other software, the only way to understand how it works is by reading the source code, which the AI does not have access to.

So when the AI connects to Formulize, it truly gets the big picture. Add a new form, change the permissions on another one, add a connection, and the AI will instantly see and understand how you have modified your application — and probably *why* you modified it that way as well.
