For most of the last twenty years, getting Formulize running well meant one of two things: doing it entirely yourself, or hiring us to design and build it with you. The first is free and always will be. The second works, but it is a big first step for someone who just wants to keep track of something.

So we are adding the step in between. **Hosted Formulize**, on our servers, from $19 a month.

## What you get

* Your own Formulize at *yoursite.formulize.net*, ready to use
* All maintenance and security updates, applied by us
* Nightly backups and 24/7 monitoring
* Unlimited users and unlimited data on every plan
* AI integration included, on every plan

Plans go from **Solo** (one form) through **Trio** (three forms) to **Bronze** (unlimited forms). Above that, Silver, Gold, Diamond and Platinum add unlimited professional support, training days, and consulting from the development team.

## The open source version is not going anywhere

Formulize stays free, GPL-2.0, and complete. There is no cut-down community edition and no feature held back for paying customers — the hosted plans are the same software, with the server work done for you. [Download it and run it yourself](/download/) any time.
