{%- comment -%} Each row carries the release's teaser under its headline, because a version number on its own tells a reader nothing about whether the release is the one they are looking for. The ten-row "Latest releases" list on the main news page carries them for the same reason, in the same markup - keep the two in step. The date, the headline and the teaser are three things, and .news-list-row lays a row out as a two-column flex: the date, then everything else. So the headline and the teaser are wrapped together in .news-text, which stacks them in the second column. The link is on the headline, not around the whole row, because a teaser is Markdown and can carry links of its own - which an enclosing would make invalid. .stretched-link puts the row back to being clickable anywhere. {%- endcomment -%}
