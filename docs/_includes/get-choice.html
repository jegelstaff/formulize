{% comment %} The three-way "how involved do you want us to be" choice, used on the homepage, on /get/ and on /download/. All three cards go to /pricing/, each to its own anchor: #do-it-yourself, #hosted, #managed. They used to go to the bare /pricing/ with no anchor, which made the choice decorative - everyone landed at the top of the same page and had to work out which third of it applied to them. The anchors are what fix that, so the card stays the decision without needing three different destinations. The kickers are the anchor names on purpose: the three coloured bands on /pricing/ are titled Do it yourself, Hosted and Managed, so a renamed kicker means a renamed band and a changed id. Managed pointed at /contact/ for a while, on the reasoning that someone wanting it built for them wants a human rather than a price list. It goes to the plan now - the Managed band carries its own "Tell us what you need" button through to /contact/, so the human is still one click away and the three cards behave alike. Prices here are the entry price of each band; the full ladder is on /pricing/ in start-rail.html. These three headlines are also where the plan names came from - the tiers on /pricing/ are called You build it, We host it, We help, We train you and We've got this. Change a headline here and the plan names have to move with it, or the homepage and the pricing page stop telling the same story. Each card is built like its band on /pricing/: the band's colour (the card-plan-* classes share their rules with plan-band-*) with the kicker sitting in it the way the band's heading does, and the details on a white panel on top. That is also what sets them apart from the plain white cards elsewhere on the homepage: those describe things you could build, these are things you can get. {% endcomment %}
