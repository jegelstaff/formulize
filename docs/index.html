Self-host it free, or let us host it for you. --- {% comment %} Band order is the order a stranger needs things in: what is it, is anything happening right now, what would I use it for, show me the AI story, what does it cost, is this alive. This used to be nine bands: a concrete "what would you build" triad immediately followed by an abstract restatement of the same pitch (Improvise/Organize/Customize - already the hero's own tagline, and spelled out in full on /features/), then a three-video row on top of the hero's own video and the AI band's video link. All of that was cut rather than trimmed, because the point of a homepage is orientation, not a teaser for every page in the nav - the nav's dropdowns (see _data/nav.yml) now carry the "let me browse" job instead. "Hire us by the hour" used to be a full band of its own; it is now a line inside "Three ways to start", since it doesn't need a stranger's full attention on the way past. News moved up from the bottom of the page to right after the hero, on the theory that "is this project alive right now" is worth answering early for a returning visitor, not just at the very end for someone who read everything else first. It also now leads with any pinned story - see news_pin on the News form, and the pinned-story sort in _plugins/news_pages.rb - so an announcement that matters can sit above its publish date without needing a fake future-dated entry to get there. News is Liquid rather than a runtime fetch - _plugins/news_pages.rb puts the stories in site.data at build time. {% endcomment %}
